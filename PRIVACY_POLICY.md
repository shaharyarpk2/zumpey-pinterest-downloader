# Privacy Policy for Zumpey.com — Pinterest Batch Extractor & Downloader

**Effective Date:** August 28, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 28, 2026  

Zumpey.com ("we", "our", or "the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how our Chrome Extension handles user data.

---

### 1. Single Purpose & Data Processing
The single purpose of **Zumpey.com - Pinterest Batch Extractor & Downloader** is to enable users to batch extract, select, organize, and download media files (high-resolution original images and 1080p MP4 videos) along with their metadata spreadsheets directly from Pinterest.

### 2. No Personal Data Collection
* **We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII)** such as your name, email address, postal address, phone number, or passwords.
* **We do NOT track your browsing history**, search queries, or activity outside of the specific Pinterest pages where you actively invoke the extension.
* **We do NOT use tracking pixels, analytics beacons, or third-party advertising scripts.**

### 3. Local Browser Storage
Any user preferences configured in the Options menu (such as custom filename patterns, folder naming tokens, export formats, or download delays) are stored strictly locally on your machine using Chrome's native `chrome.storage.sync` API. This data never leaves your browser ecosystem.

### 4. Permissions Usage
* **`downloads`:** Used solely to trigger browser downloads of the selected Pinterest images, 1080p MP4 videos, generated ZIP archives, or Excel (.xlsx/CSV) spreadsheets requested by the user.
* **`storage`:** Used to save your download configuration locally.
* **`activeTab` & `scripting`:** Used solely to inspect and index pin elements on the active Pinterest tab when requested.
* **Host Permissions (`*://*.pinterest.com/*`, `*://*.pinimg.com/*`):** Used strictly to crawl pin elements and fetch media stream buffers directly from Pinterest CDN servers.

### 5. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, trade, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties.
